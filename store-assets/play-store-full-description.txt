ShellGuard-TOTP is a privacy-first, offline-autonomous two-factor authentication (2FA) companion built to protect your digital identity. Engineered with zero-knowledge hardware encryption and an offline-first architecture, ShellGuard-TOTP generates standard RFC 6238 verification codes directly on your device—with zero tracking, zero cloud telemetry, and zero mandatory accounts.

Whether securing your personal email, developer accounts, cloud infrastructure, or gaming logins, ShellGuard-TOTP keeps your authentication secrets sealed behind Android KeyStore hardware protection.


KEY FEATURES & CAPABILITIES

ZERO-KNOWLEDGE HARDWARE ENCRYPTION
• Hardware-Backed Protection: Master keys are generated and isolated using the Android KeyStore (StrongBox / TEE) with AES-256-GCM.
• Encrypted At Rest: Secrets are stored inside a dedicated SQLCipher database with full disk encryption.
• Privacy Shield: Built-in FLAG_SECURE window protection shields your 2FA tokens from task-switcher previews and unauthorized screen captures.
• Biometric & PIN Vault Lock: Guard access to your codes with biometric authentication (fingerprint / face unlock) or custom Master PIN/Password.

REAL-TIME COUNTDOWN & CLEAN ERGONOMICS
• Dynamic Countdown Rings: Visual timer rings smoothly deplete and transition color as codes cycle, giving you instant situational awareness.
• Fluid Speed Dial: Access live camera scanning, gallery screenshot import, and manual entry in a single tap.
• Staggered Card View: Responsive cards with high-contrast split digits and one-tap clipboard copy with auto-clearing clipboard buffer.
• Instant Search: Quickly locate tokens across your vault by account, issuer, or category.

RAPID ONBOARDING & QR IMPORT
• CameraX QR Scanner: High-performance camera scanner instantly detects and imports standard otpauth:// QR codes.
• Gallery Screenshot QR Decoder: Import 2FA setup QR codes directly from screenshots saved in your device gallery using Google ML Kit.
• Manual Secret Setup: Intuitive manual entry supporting custom issuers, accounts, digits, intervals, and algorithms.

UNIVERSAL MIGRATION & BACKUP
• One-Tap Habitat Import: Seamlessly migrate from Bitwarden Vaults, Aegis Authenticator, 2FAS, Google Authenticator, or Steam Guard with zero data leakage.
• Encrypted Vault Backups: Export password-protected .sgtotp.bak backup archives for secure, offline device transfers.

STEAM GUARD & ALGORITHM FLEXIBILITY
• Steam Guard Authenticator: Built-in generation for Steam's 5-character alphanumeric TOTP codes and steam:// URI schemas.
• Cryptographic Standards: Full support for HMAC-SHA1, HMAC-SHA256, and HMAC-SHA512 hashing algorithms.

REEF MODERNIST DESIGN SYSTEM
• Bioluminescent Marine Accents: Personalize your vault with 6 curated palette themes (Reef Pink, Cyan Vent, Purple Shell, Emerald Trench, Amber Flare, and Monochrome).
• Deep Dark & Light Modes: Tailored contrast ratios built specifically for AMOLED displays and high-visibility environments.

OPTIONAL SELF-HOSTED SYNC
• Run your own server? ShellGuard-TOTP supports optional, read-only one-way mirror synchronization with self-hosted ShellGuard servers using end-to-end encrypted ShellCryption envelopes.
• No cloud server required: 100% of all authenticator functions operate completely offline without any network dependency.


YOUR PRIVACY IS ABSOLUTE
• No account registration or email required.
• No advertisements or tracking frameworks.
• No internet access required for core authenticator functions.
• Modern Android 16 (API 36) & 16 KB kernel page-size optimized.
